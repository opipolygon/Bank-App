About RKPL

Bangladesh's Leading Shell Lubricant Distributor

Ranks Petroleum Limited (RKPL), a prominent SBU of Rancon, has been the official Macro Distributor of Shell Lubricants in Bangladesh since 2004. We bridge the global excellence of Shell with local expertise.

Through a robust distribution network spanning all 64 districts, RKPL delivers the right lubricant to every customer — from individual motorists to large industrial operations.
